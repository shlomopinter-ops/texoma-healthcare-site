What we do

One loop from bedside reading to chart

Our platform connects the vitals your staff already take with a remote nursing team that watches for change, so abnormal readings reach the right person quickly and every step is documented.

Vitals at the bedside

Blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, temperature, weight and glucose are captured on a tablet or connected device during normal rounds.

Abnormal-vitals alerts

Readings outside each resident's set ranges are flagged right away instead of waiting to be noticed on paper or in a later review.

Nurse-reviewed

A nurse reviews each flagged reading in context before the facility is alerted, with acknowledgement and escalation tracked.

Written back to the EHR

Readings and review notes flow into the resident's electronic health record. Designed to integrate with leading long-term-care EHRs.

Who it's for

Built around the people who care for residents

Facilities

Skilled nursing and assisted living communities that want consistent monitoring between rounds and a clear record of every reading and response.

Nursing teams

Less double entry and fewer missed changes. Staff take vitals once, and the follow-up is tracked from alert to resolution.

Medical directors

Visibility into trends and alerts across residents, with documented nurse review to support clinical decisions.

Roadmap

From monitoring to whole-person care

Remote patient monitoring is where we start. We are building toward care-management programs on the same foundation.

  1. Now

    Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)

    Bedside vitals, abnormal-reading alerts, nurse review and EHR write-back.

  2. Planned

    Advanced Primary Care Management (APCM)

    Ongoing, coordinated primary care management for residents with chronic conditions.

  3. Planned

    Collaborative Care (CoCM)

    Team-based behavioral health care linking primary care, care managers and psychiatric consultation.

  4. Planned

    Behavioral Health Integration (BHI)

    Behavioral health support integrated into each resident's regular care.
