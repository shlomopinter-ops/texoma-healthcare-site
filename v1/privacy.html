Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Texoma Healthcare Group ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information connected with this website and with our platform.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or contact forms. We do not collect personal information through it. If you email us, we use your message and address only to respond to you.

Protected health information

When we provide services to healthcare facilities, we handle protected health information (PHI) as a business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and our business associate agreements with those facilities. We use and disclose PHI only as permitted by those agreements and by law, and only the minimum necessary to deliver care and services.

How we protect information

Our platform is designed for HIPAA compliance, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls and audit logging.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or PHI. We share information only with the facilities and care teams we serve, with service providers bound by appropriate agreements, or when required by law.

Residents and patients

If you are a resident or patient, your facility controls your health record. Please contact your facility to access or correct your information, or to ask about how it is used.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
